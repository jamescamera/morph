    02 / Process

    Three steps.
    No timeline.

    1. I.

      Collect

      Any stills — photos, renders, scans. Different sizes are fine; Morph crops each to fill the frame.

    2. II.

      Flow

      A shared noise field drags both images through one another, so pixels travel instead of fade.

    3. III.

      Keep

      Export records the canvas straight to MP4 or WebM. Nothing is uploaded, ever.
