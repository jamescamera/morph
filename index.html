    02 / Process

    How a face
    becomes another.

    1. I.

      Find

      478 landmarks per face (brows, lids, lips, jaw) are located in your browser with an on-device model.

    2. II.

      Line up

      Every face is rotated and scaled so the eyes sit on the same line, then the points are triangulated into a mesh of about a thousand triangles.

    3. III.

      Warp & dissolve

      Each triangle slides from the first face's shape to the second's while the pixels cross-dissolve. Toggle Show mesh to watch it happen.

    03 / Styles

    No face?
    Melt it instead.

    Rooms, streets and objects don't have landmarks, so four melt styles drag one image through the next along a drifting noise field instead.

    Liquid

    Ink in water. Edges bleed along a drifting current.

    Swirl

    The center twists open like a drain and pulls the next frame through.

    Luma

    Highlights go first, shadows last. Made for black & white.

    Shatter

    The frame cracks into shards that flip one by one.
